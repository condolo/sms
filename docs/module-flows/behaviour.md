# Behaviour and Pastoral

## Record a merit or incident

1. Open **Behaviour → Incidents** and choose merit or demerit.
2. Select the configured category and behaviour; confirm the point value.
3. Select the student and date. Add the required explanation for high-value incidents and any helpful context.
4. Submit, then review the record in the register and ensure the status is correct.

## Resolve an appeal

Students submit an appeal against an eligible demerit with a reason. Parents can add context to their child's appeal. Authorized staff review the case, record a resolution note, and accept, reject, or escalate where permitted.

Use categories, milestones, intervention thresholds, houses, and detention settings under the authorized settings workflow. Changing a category does not rewrite historical incidents.


## Setup, updates, and verification

### Maintain behaviour configuration and records

Before recording incidents, ask an authorized administrator to review categories, point values, milestones, thresholds, houses, and detention settings. When revising a category, remember the change governs future use and does not rewrite historical incidents. To correct a record or appeal, open the original item and use its available edit/review action; retain the resolution note and verify the updated status in the register. Escalate unclear or disputed cases through the school's pastoral process.

**Access:** Behaviour module permissions and student/class scope apply. Appeals, escalation, and administration settings have specific restrictions.
