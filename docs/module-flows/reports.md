# Reports & Analytics

## View and export a report

1. Open **Reports** and choose a report area (for example academic, attendance, behaviour, finance, or enrollment).
2. Set the available class, subject, year/term, and date-range filters.
3. Confirm the displayed scope before interpreting totals; teacher views may be limited to assigned classes while leadership views may cover the school.
4. Use CSV export only after confirming the selected filters and that the export is authorized for its intended recipient.

Reports are derived from their source modules. If a figure looks wrong, validate the underlying records and selected period before treating the report as an official record.


## Setup, updates, and verification

### Configure, export, and investigate reports

Choose the report and set every relevant period, class, and status filter before interpreting results. Compare a sample total with its source module when figures look unexpected. Before export, verify the selected scope and intended recipient, then store the file securely and remove local copies when no longer needed. If the report is incomplete, check source permissions and record scope as well as filters; do not broaden access just to make a report populate.

**Access:** Reports permissions govern report viewing and export. Data-specific permissions and scopes may further restrict individual report sections.
