Which of these you see depends on what your school has switched on — see §1.

## 1. What You See Depends on Your School

The sidebar you see is not the same for every school. Each section — Grades & Reports, Attendance, Fee Statements, Timetable, Library, Messages, School Calendar, Behaviour & Rewards — only appears if your child's school has that module switched on. **Overview** and **Announcements** are always there; everything else is the school's choice, made in their own admin settings, not something you can turn on or off yourself.

So if a section this guide describes isn't in your sidebar, it isn't broken — your school simply hasn't enabled that feature. Contact the school office if you expected to see one.

The portal also picks up your school's own name, logo, and brand colours automatically — you're not looking at a generic Msingi screen, you're looking at *your* school's portal, even though every school runs on the same underlying system.

---

## 2. Signing In

Your school issues your parent account — typically a username/email and a temporary password, often shared when your child is enrolled or when a sibling is added to your account. Go to the school's Msingi login page and sign in with those credentials.

If you have more than one child at the same school, one account covers all of them — see §14.

If you've forgotten your password or your account won't let you in, contact the school office; they manage parent account resets, not a public "forgot password" self-service flow.

## 3. Your Dashboard Layout

The portal has two parts:

- **Sidebar (left)** — your navigation. **My Child** groups the sections about your child specifically (Overview, Grades & Reports, Attendance, Fee Statements, Timetable, Library); **School** groups the ones about the school as a whole (Messages, School Calendar, Behaviour & Rewards, Announcements). Click any item to open that page — each one is a full page on its own, not just a scroll down a long list.
- **Top bar** — a greeting, the current term and academic year, a notification bell, and two buttons: **Message School** and, if your school has M-Pesa payments switched on, **Pay Fees via M-Pesa**. Both jump straight to the relevant page.

**On a phone or narrow screen**, the sidebar tucks away behind a menu button (☰) in the top-left — tap it to open, and it closes itself once you pick something.

**On a desktop**, you can collapse the sidebar down to just icons using the **Collapse** button near the top of the sidebar — useful if you want more room for the page itself. Click the same spot (now showing an arrow) to expand it again; your choice is remembered next time you visit.

---

## 4. Overview

Your landing page. Four quick cards at the top — **Attendance**, **Average**, **Fee Balance**, **Messages** — each one a shortcut straight into its full page when you click it.

Below that, whatever's immediately relevant today: **Today's Timetable** (if your school uses Timetable), **This Week's Snapshot** (a downloadable PDF summary — attendance, behaviour, assignments — generated automatically every Saturday, once your child has had at least one), and a peek at **Upcoming Exams** if any are scheduled soon.

---

## 5. Grades & Reports

Everything about your child's academic results, in one place:

- **Subject-by-subject scores**, each with a progress bar and the letter grade. This reflects your child's most recently **published** report card — not day-to-day marks as teachers enter them. Parents don't have access to live, in-progress marks; only an officially published report.
- **Term-over-Term Trend** — a small chart of your child's overall average across past terms, once there's more than one to compare.
- **Report Cards** — download any published report as a PDF. If your school requires a minimum fee-clearance percentage before releasing report cards and your account hasn't reached it yet, you'll see **Locked** here with the percentage you still need — clear the balance (§7) and it unlocks.
- **Upcoming Exams** — scheduled exams for your child's class, soonest first.
- **Curriculum Coverage** — how much of this term's syllabus has actually been taught so far, per subject (not a grade — a teaching-progress percentage).

> Some early-years classes use a different grading style entirely — indicators and performance bands instead of a percentage score. If your child's report card looks like that, it's not an error; it's how your school assesses that class, and this page shows it in that format automatically.

---

## 6. Attendance

This term's attendance at a glance — Present, Absent, Late, and Total Days, each with its own tally and a small bar showing it as a share of the term. Below that, a **Recent Records** list of the last several days, each marked Present, Absent, or Late.

---

## 7. Fee Statements

Your child's fee position for the current term: total fees, amount paid, and outstanding balance (or a structured line-by-line breakdown, if your school issues one). A clear **Cleared** or **Balance Due** badge sits at the top.

- If there's a balance, you'll see the due date and — only if your school has M-Pesa payments configured — a note pointing you to the **Pay Fees via M-Pesa** button in the top bar. If your school hasn't set up M-Pesa, that button simply won't appear anywhere in the portal; pay through whatever method your school has told you to use instead.
- **Recent Payments** lists your last few payments, with the M-Pesa reference code where one applies.

---

## 8. Timetable

Your child's full schedule for **today** — subject, time, room, and teacher, with the current lesson highlighted and the next one marked. If your school has switched on Emergency Online Learning for the day, you'll see a banner saying so at the top.

---

## 9. Library

Books your child currently has borrowed, with due dates. A book that's overdue or reported lost is flagged clearly, with how many days overdue.

---

## 10. Messages

Your real inbox — direct messages sent to you personally, messages sent to all parents, and school-wide announcements, most recent first. This is the same inbox the notification bell's badge count is about: an unread count there means an unread message here, and opening a message here marks it read everywhere, including that badge.

Clicking a message marks it read. Msingi doesn't yet support writing directly back from this screen — for anything needing a reply, use **Message School** in the top bar, or contact the school office directly.

---

## 11. School Calendar

Upcoming school-wide events — sports days, holidays, meetings — soonest first, with how many days away each one is.

---

## 12. Behaviour & Rewards

Your child's behaviour points total, their current badge (Bronze, Silver, or Gold, based on points earned), their most recent reward if any, and the latest teacher comment, if one has been added.

---

## 13. Announcements

The full list of notices from your school, most recent first — the same feed your Overview and Messages pages draw their previews from, shown here in full with the complete text of each one.

---

## 14. If You Have More Than One Child

If more than one of your children attends the same school on your account, a child switcher appears above your name at the bottom of the sidebar. Click it to see all your children and pick which one's dashboard you're viewing — every page (Grades, Attendance, Fees, etc.) switches to show that child's data.

Each child's fee balance, grades, and attendance are tracked completely separately — switching children never mixes their records together.

---

## 15. Notifications

The bell icon in the top bar shows a count when you have unread notifications. Click it to see what's new; selecting one takes you to the relevant page (usually Messages).

---

## 16. Signing Out

Click the sign-out icon next to your name at the bottom of the sidebar. This ends your session — you'll need your password again to sign back in.

---

## 17. Something Looks Wrong?

- **A section you expected isn't in the sidebar** — your school hasn't enabled that module yet (see §1). Ask the school office, not Msingi support, since this is a per-school setting.
- **Grades look out of date** — you're seeing the most recently *published* report card, not live in-progress marks; the school publishes a new one at the end of each assessment cycle.
- **Report card download is locked** — your account's fee-clearance percentage is below what the school requires for release; see §7.
- **No "Pay Fees via M-Pesa" button** — your school hasn't configured M-Pesa payments; use their alternative payment method.
- **Anything else** — contact your school's office; they manage parent accounts and can escalate a genuine system issue on your behalf.
